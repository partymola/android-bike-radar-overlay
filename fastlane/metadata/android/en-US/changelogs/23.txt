Home Assistant entity ids are renamed from varia_ to bikeradar_, and the MQTT topics from varia/ to bikeradar/. The app retires the old entities once the new ones reach your Home Assistant, but your automations and any raw-topic triggers must be repointed by hand, and the new entities start with no history. If you cleared your Home Assistant configuration on an older version, clear it once more: credentials in an older format survived the first clear. Beep tiers now use true range, so there are fewer top-tier beeps. An ordinary beep can no longer silence the urgent warning. Approaching cars are coloured by closing speed, and a re-paired radar reconnects instead of staying dead. You can now enter coordinates by hand for the light auto-mode, and your approximate location no longer reaches the system log.
