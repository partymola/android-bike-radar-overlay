Home Assistant MQTT topics move from varia/ to bikeradar/, so anything you trigger on a raw topic needs repointing by hand. Your entity ids keep the naming they have, but every entity is registered afresh and some come back with a _2 on the end; anything pointing at an affected id stops working until you delete the stale entity and rename the _2 back. Check your battery sensors, the close-pass event, and, if you have the camera, the front light mode. The rebuilt entities start with no history. If you cleared your Home Assistant setup on an older version, clear it once more: older-format credentials survived the first clear. Beep tiers now use true range, so there are fewer top-tier beeps. An ordinary beep can no longer silence the urgent warning. A re-paired radar reconnects instead of staying dead. You can now enter coordinates by hand for the light auto-mode, and your approximate location no longer reaches the system log.
