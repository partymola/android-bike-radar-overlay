Directional alert audio has been removed, so if you had it on your cues are mono again: it made no audible difference through a phone speaker, and on headphones it turned the delayed Bluetooth copy into an echo. The urgent warning now judges whether a vehicle will clear your whole bike rather than just the radar, so a car converging on your front wheel no longer scores as clear; the margin is yours to set in Settings. The app no longer dies in the background after you revoke its Bluetooth permission. Your accessories' Bluetooth addresses stay out of release logs, so a bug report you share no longer carries them. Settings shows coordinates you typed on the Permissions screen too, the location cards say that typed coordinates override GPS, and distances are written the way your language writes them. Spanish has had a thorough pass for grammar, gender and register.
