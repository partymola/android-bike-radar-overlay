If your radar is not a RearVue 820, this release is aimed at you: the app used to connect, find nothing it recognised and give up, and it now falls back to the older stream instead. You get the approach beeps, the all-clear, and overlay colours that show distance instead of speed. You do not get the urgent warning or close-pass counting, as those need the newer protocol the 820 uses. Nobody has confirmed this on real hardware yet, so a works or doesn't-work report is the most useful thing you can send. A radar that never finishes connecting can now be recorded from the Debug screen, and a capture log can be shared without ending the ride. Every screen finally uses the same words for your devices, and the app stops calling a working radar unpaired. The dead-radar sound now stands in on traffic seen just before the link died; it is on by default and can sound after you park near a busy road, so turn it off in Settings if it does. New app icon.
