Another app on your phone can now use your radar if you let it: it reads what the radar sees, and with a second tick can switch your tail light, or hide the overlay if you allow it both. Nothing reaches it until you say yes on a screen naming the app, and Settings takes it back. False urgent warnings are fixed: when the radar stopped reporting a vehicle's sideways position, the app could read that as dead behind you and predict a collision with a car that was never on that line. You can also tell the app a ride is over, so the drop alert stops guessing from traffic while you lock up, and on a range-only radar with no eBike you can set how far back that alert looks for traffic, from 30 seconds to an hour. Close-pass counting no longer counts a pass the radar could not measure sideways, so the count reads lower and any Home Assistant automation using it fires less often; a related fix makes overtakes and time in traffic read higher.
