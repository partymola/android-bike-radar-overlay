Close passes are now measured where the vehicle is actually beside you. The clearance used to be taken from any moment the radar was following a vehicle, and a car directly behind reads as dead centre, so passes were often logged at a few centimetres that never happened. A sideways reading of exactly zero is no longer taken as a clearance either, and the tightest pass in your ride history follows the same rules. Expect fewer close passes and wider clearances, and check any Home Assistant automation that triggers on them. Turning the dashcam switch off now keeps the camera you picked, and Settings shows which one is saved and lets you clear it. The app now connects to read the battery only of a radar or the dashcam you are using, where it used to connect to any paired accessory it recognised. A Home Assistant battery sensor for any other accessory stops updating and keeps its last value.
