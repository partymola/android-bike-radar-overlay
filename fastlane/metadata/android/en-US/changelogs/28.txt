One screen now appears the first time you open this version, once and not again, saying what the radar can and cannot do: that the app warns you about traffic behind you and cannot look behind for you, that it can miss a vehicle or warn late, that the radar link or the sound can drop without notice, that "road clear" only means the radar sees nothing, and to always look behind before you move out. A tap is all it asks for, and the same text is under Settings, About, Before you ride whenever you want it again. The project page gains a Known limitations section covering the same ground at more length. Wording that described the overlay as sitting over a map or a navigation app now says "the app underneath", in both languages. Nothing about the overlay or any alert changed.
