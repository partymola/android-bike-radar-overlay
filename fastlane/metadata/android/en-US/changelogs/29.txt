The app no longer thinks you are still cornering long after a corner. That could hold back the "road clear" chime for minutes, and a vehicle close behind you on the straight that followed could go without its beep. Now the turn usually ends soon after the corner does. The cost: a slow, sweeping bend can stop counting as a turn while you are still in it, so "road clear" can sound before it ends. No change to Home Assistant or to which radars work.
