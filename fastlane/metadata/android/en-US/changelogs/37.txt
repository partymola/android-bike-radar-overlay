With a Bosch eBike, the app warns you if you ride without the rear radar: the drop alert and a "No rear radar" banner. The overlay draws each vehicle to scale from its front, and keeps its shape in landscape. A new "Warn up to" setting keeps the fastest phantom readings quiet. A vehicle far to the side can no longer hide a real car's beeps. Debug screenshots are capped at 30, and the Privacy screen says where ride history and logs are kept.
