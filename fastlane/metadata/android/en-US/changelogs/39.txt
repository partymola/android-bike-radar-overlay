A radar that connects but never sends data now counts as off, so the drop alert and the warning for riding without it can sound. Close passes are measured where the vehicle draws level, and readings closing faster than 126 km/h are left out of close passes and peak closing speed. Switching eBike data on or off takes effect at once. New in Experimental: hold the urgent warning for vehicles to the side.
