New: the walk-away alarm sounds if you walk off with the radar still on. After a car passes you, the app now beeps for the car behind it, so fewer cars pass you without a sound. The radar now works without the Notifications permission, the dashcam's walk-away alarm stops when the radar comes back, and light auto-mode uses your area instead of London, even if you don't open the app before you ride. Plus fixes for pairing and permissions.
